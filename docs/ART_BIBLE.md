# WHODUNIT?! — Art Bible (draft v0.1)

## 1. Overall Aesthetic

WHODUNIT?! should feel like a **classic 1940s theatrical slapstick cartoon detective short** — rubbery, loud, fast and funny — rendered as an **original** property.

- **Line:** thick, confident black ink outlines of varying weight; heavier on outer silhouettes, lighter on interior detail.
- **Shape language:** strong, instantly readable silhouettes; exaggerated proportions; rubber-hose-era *energy* with squash-and-stretch in every pose.
- **Character color:** flat, saturated fills, minimal cel shading (one shadow tone max), high contrast. Characters are always **brighter and more saturated than backgrounds**.
- **Backgrounds:** dark, moody mansion interiors — muted desaturated palette (deep plums, bottle greens, oxblood, umber), dramatic raking shadows, a thunderstorm raging outside the windows (lightning flashes, rain streaks), and pools of warm amber interior lamp light.
- **Staging:** theatrical, like a stage set; characters read clearly against the background at a glance.

### Originality rule (non-negotiable)
All designs must be **ORIGINAL**. Never copy, trace, or imitate existing copyrighted characters, logos, or designs — **no Looney Tunes, Disney, Fleischer, MGM, Warner, or any other studio's characters**. We capture the *era's energy* (timing, exaggeration, ink-and-paint look), never its specific characters, faces, costumes, or trademarks. Do not use studio or character names in generation prompts.

Prepend this text **verbatim** to every character generation:

```
Original 1940s-style theatrical slapstick cartoon character art, hand-inked ink-and-paint animation cel look, thick bold black ink outlines, strong readable silhouette, exaggerated squash-and-stretch proportions, flat saturated colors with minimal simple cel shading, high contrast, clean crisp linework, expressive rubbery cartoon acting, original character design. Full body, single character only, centered, facing three-quarters toward the viewer, entire figure visible from top of head to bottom of shoes with empty margin around it, standing on nothing, isolated on a plain flat solid pure green (#00FF00) background with no shadow, no floor, no gradient, no scenery.
```

## 7. Asset Pipeline Notes

### Repo layout (canonical paths)
- Sprites: `assets/characters/<id>/<emotion>.webp` — ids `reginald`, `victoria`, `archibald`, `gregory`; emotions `neutral`, `talking`, `angry`, `nervous`, `shocked`, `smug`, `sad` (e.g. `assets/characters/gregory/nervous.webp`).
- This bible: `docs/ART_BIBLE.md`.
- Drafting workspace (not shipped): raw generations in `toon-drafts/raw/`, review sheets `toon-drafts/characters/lineup_sheet.png`, `toon-drafts/characters/silhouette_test.png`, `toon-drafts/characters/reginald/reginald_contact_sheet.png`, scripts in `toon-drafts/tools/`.

### Sprite spec (all characters) — v0.2
- RGBA lossless WebP, canvas **784 × 1224** (widened from 482 in v0.2 so Archibald fits at full size), feet on baseline **y = 1200**, strictly horizontally centered on the feet (feet center x = 392). No clamping needed at these scales.
- Canvas width rule: smallest multiple of 16 that fits Archibald at 0.78× Reginald's height in all 7 poses with ≥12 px side padding (he needs 774 px, driven by his widest off-center poses → 784).
- Feet center = midpoint of the occupied columns in the bottom 10% of the figure's height (catches both shoes even when one is raised).
- **All characters face LEFT** (three-quarter view, nose toward the viewer's left). Mirror in-engine for right-facing.
- Sweat drops stay white (`#FFFFFF`) — the `#7FD3FF` value above is for effect overlays only.
- **Relative scale** is baked into the sprites: one uniform scale per character across all 7 poses (sprites never change size when swapping emotion). Machine-readable copy: `toon-drafts/characters/scale_report.json`.

|---|---|---|---|

| victoria | 0.9115 | 0.92 | fits, no cap |
| archibald | 0.8583 | 0.78 | sets the canvas width |
| gregory | 0.6893 | 0.62 | fits, no cap; clearly shorter than Archibald |

### Prompt assembly
`LOCKED STYLE PROMPT` + `LOCKED CHARACTER DESCRIPTION` + pose line + facing line + `LOCKED NEGATIVE`. Facing line (all characters except Reginald, whose reference already faced left):
```
Facing direction: his/her body and face are turned three-quarters toward the LEFT side of the image (nose pointing to the viewer's left).
```
Non-neutral poses use the xAI image edit endpoint with that character's approved neutral as the reference, prefixed by: "Redraw the exact same character from the reference image — identical design, face, hair, outfit, colors, proportions, line style, scale and facing direction — in a new pose."
- Generator: xAI `grok-imagine-image-2.0`. Keying: Pillow/numpy chroma key (soft alpha ramp on G−max(R,B), green despill).
